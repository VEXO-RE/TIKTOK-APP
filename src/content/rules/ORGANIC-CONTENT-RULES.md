# ShopinistaMeta Organic Content Rules

The automation exists to support editorial work, not artificial growth.

Allowed automation:

- detect relevant product launches;
- detect new Tiendanube products;
- maintain editorial calendar;
- prepare campaign concepts;
- prepare scripts;
- prepare captions;
- prepare creative briefs;
- prepare images/video;
- create publishing drafts;
- schedule approved publications;
- analyze organic content performance;
- recommend improvements.

Not allowed:

- automated follow/unfollow;
- fake followers;
- fake engagement;
- unsolicited comment automation;
- mass direct messages;
- spam;
- engagement manipulation;
- publishing outside configured brand rules.

Publishing safety:

- default = draft/review;
- automatic publishing must require an explicit feature flag;
- supported platform capability must exist;
- authorization must be valid;
- content must pass validation rules.

Brand objective:

Organic content should support:

TikTok
  -> ShopinistaMeta
  -> Tiendanube product/category/landing
  -> WhatsApp when appropriate
  -> measurable conversion.
